## Measurement concept

A fiber‑optic probe (ATR, Raman or transmission) would be positioned at the fermentation line to collect emitted or scattered light, with the fiber link carrying the signal to the existing spectrometer; the exact probe geometry and fiber type must match the chosen spectral range [S01 Table 1 (p.6)].

## Spectral range

Mid‑IR ATR: 4000–400 cm⁻¹ (2.5–25 µm); Raman: 400–1100 nm (visible to short‑wave NIR); Near‑IR transmission: 780–2500 nm. The compatible fiber link for each range still needs confirmation.

## Risks, limitations and uncertainty

- Sample state is unknown, so probe suitability cannot be guaranteed [S01 Table 1 (p.6)]
- Optical variability (turbidity, bubbles) may impair transmission or scattering measurements
